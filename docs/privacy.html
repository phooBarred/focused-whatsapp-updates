Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy covers Focused WhatsApp, Android package app.quietupdates, maintained through the Focused WhatsApp downloads project.

Screen information stays on your phone

The accessibility service examines relevant screen controls and text to recognize protected sections and settings. Screen content is processed in memory. The app does not upload or keep a history of messages, screen text, screenshots or browsing activity. Protection settings and the local daily-usage counter are stored on the phone.

Google account and backup access

Google sign-in and backup are optional. When you connect an account, the app uses its email address and a Drive account identifier to associate backup operations with that account. Those connection details stay on the phone. Google Play services handles authorization; access tokens used by the app stay in memory.

The app requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata to read and write its own configuration file in your Google Drive's hidden app storage. It does not request access to all your Drive files. Connecting an account alone does not upload settings: choose Back up now or Restore backup before scheduled backups can begin.

What a backup contains

Backups contain the waiting period, access-window preference, protection plans, schedules, daily allowances, unlock method, appearance and backup schedule. If a password is configured, the backup includes its salt and derived password verifier, never the plaintext password. Treat that verifier and the configuration as sensitive.

Backups exclude protection on/off, active waits, queued changes, open access windows, today's usage, phone permissions, sign-in tokens, screen content and messages. Restoring requires protection off or an open access window and cannot replace an existing different password. Android automatic backup and device-transfer copying are disabled.

Storage, sharing and retention

Google stores the backup in your account with its account and app access controls. This is not end-to-end encrypted backup. The app reuses a small configuration file; Google may retain file revisions under its own policies. App traffic to Google and GitHub uses HTTPS.

The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or analytics, or use it to train AI models. Google data is used only to provide the backup and restore features described here, in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Versions through 0.7.4 used Firebase storage under the earlier project's administration. Version 0.7.5 used Drive through a shared Google project. Existing copies are not automatically imported, moved or deleted when storage or authorization projects change.

Your controls

Use Settings → Google backup to change the schedule or sign out. Signing out stops this phone's backup connection but does not delete settings or cloud copies. You can revoke the app's access in your Google Account. Google Drive's Manage apps settings can delete hidden app data for a connected app; deleting data under an older shared app identity may also affect other apps using that identity. Ask for help before deleting a shared copy you need.

Updates, website and support

Manual update checks and APK downloads connect to GitHub. Google and GitHub process the network information needed to provide their services under their own policies: Google and GitHub. This documentation site has no added analytics, advertising scripts, forms or tracking cookies.

For questions, use the support page on GitHub. Issues are public: describe the problem without posting backup files, passwords, account tokens or private messages. Material changes to these practices will be reflected in this policy and, where applicable, release notes.
